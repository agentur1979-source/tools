Why so few agents guarantee a deadline

You can only promise a deadline if the price is honest, the documents are ready in advance and someone follows the sale every day. We do this before the listing goes live, so we back the result with our own money.

A typical sale

  • “We’ll do our best”, with no deadline and no consequences
  • The price is set by wishful thinking, then cut for months
  • Documents are gathered only once a buyer appears
  • You hear about viewings only if you call yourself
  • The fee is the same however long it takes

A guaranteed sale

  • The 60-day deadline is written into the contract
  • Price from the audit and real sales nearby
  • A 12-point check before the first viewing

The apartment had residents on record who had never lived there

The owner was selling his apartment and buying a new one at the same time. When we applied for deregistration, the register still showed his relatives as residents, although they had never been registered there. A technical error, but the buyer sees strangers in the documents, and a chain of two deals can collapse.

We filed a request with the Interior Ministry, proved the error and had it corrected before closing. Both deals held, and the apartment sold in 30 days. Since then we order the residents’ register on day one, not just before the deal.

Questions

When do the 60 days start?

From the day the listing goes live at the price agreed after the audit until the contract with the buyer is signed. The exact terms, including what counts as meeting the guarantee, are set out in the contract.

What if I disagree with the audited price?

We can market it at your price for two weeks. If the market doesn’t respond, we switch to the audited price and the guarantee starts from that day. We can also work without the guarantee on standard terms.

What happens after 60 days if it hasn’t sold?

We keep working until it sells, and the fee drops from 3% to 1.5%. No upfront payments and no extra charges.

Why only 5 apartments a month?

Keeping the deadline means working on each apartment every day. More listings would mean less attention for each, and we back the result with our money.

Can I sell if I don’t live in Moscow?

Yes. We handle the viewings, you see everything in the owner portal, and a power of attorney is enough for the deal. We explain how to issue it safely. Jürgen Schmidt is German himself and speaks German and Russian.

Who pays for marketing and preparation?

We do. Photos, video, listing promotion and the legal check are included in the fee you pay only after the sale.
